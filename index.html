      Quick tips for new students

      • Enter through Gate 1, the main gate. Cross the main road using the overpass near Gate 2.
      • Most enrolment steps happen in the Administrative Office Building.
      • Tap any building number on the map to see its offices and hours.
      • Use Get directions from any gate and follow the moving dot.
      • Switch to Night to see which windows are lit — handy for evening classes.
